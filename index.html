What Mappr is

A map that keeps up with you.

Your notes are already a map, they just look like a list. Paste an indented outline and Mappr reads the indentation as depth, builds the branch and places it. Copy a branch back and it arrives as markdown, so nothing you make here is trapped here.

notes.md on your clipboard 
# Thesis
- Evidence
  - Two trials
- Counter
→ paste Cmd V
← copy Cmd C
map networks story 4 nodes

Tabs, spaces, bullets, numbers and markdown headings all read as depth. A stripped copy with no indentation left in it still works: bold lines read as headings, a line ending in a colon introduces what follows.

What we will not do

Fast, light, private.

Diagram tools grow by adding. Every release brings another panel, another menu, another thing to load before you can draw a box. Mappr is built the other way round, and these three lines are what we check a feature against before it ships.

01

Fast

The point of Mappr is how quickly a thought becomes a branch. Every control is a key, the layout tidies itself, and the camera follows. Nothing asks you to stop and arrange.

A feature that adds a step between thinking and typing does not ship.
02

Light

The whole app is one file with nothing to install and nothing to load from anywhere else. Open it once and it works on a plane, in a classroom with bad wifi, or with the network off entirely.

No dependencies. No sign up. No loading screen.
03

Private

Your maps are stored by your browser, on your machine. There is no account, no server and nothing to upload, so nobody can read your work, including us.

Local is not the same as backed up. Export anything you cannot lose.
Use Mappr, free Free forever, and open source. Nothing is held back for a paid tier, because there is not one.

How it works

Four keys is most of it.

There are sixty-one keys in Mappr and you will never need most of them. These four build a map. Press ? inside the app for the rest.

Cmd →

Make a branch

Any arrow works. Press the same direction again from the new node to keep going that way.

Tab

Move around

Steps through the nodes on the level you are on. Arrows move between levels.

Space

Rename

Retypes whatever is selected. An arrow key leaves typing and starts moving again.

Cmd 3

Present it

Walks the map branch by branch in front of a room. No deck, no export.

Where your maps live

In your browser's own storage, on this device. No account, no server, nothing uploaded, nothing to delete later.
