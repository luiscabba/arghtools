What this is

Three rules, on all of them.

Every tool on the shelf is checked against these before it ships. They are the reason a tool here stays small while the thing it replaces keeps growing.

One job

A tool does one thing and stops. When it turns out to need a second job, it becomes a second tool rather than another panel on this one.

One page

Nothing to install, no account, no loading screen. A tool opens in a tab, does its work and closes. It keeps working with the network off.

Yours

Your work is stored by your browser, on your machine. There is no server to upload it to, so nobody can read it, including us. Export anything you cannot lose.
